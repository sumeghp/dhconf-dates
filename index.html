Dates come from each conference's official call for papers. Announced means published on the official site. Provisional means marked tentative or taken from a third-party listing. Estimated means the call isn't out yet and the date follows last year's pattern. Always check the conference website before planning around a date.

Deadlines without a stated time zone are treated as 23:59 Anywhere on Earth (UTC−12). Stars are saved in this browser.
